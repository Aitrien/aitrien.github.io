This is the first entry in the writing collection, so it gets the first catalogue number. Numbers live in front matter rather than coming from the date or the address, so they survive a rename.

## How it's built

The site is generated by [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and served from GitHub Pages. Its look comes from a separate repository, MotifSpec, imported as a Hugo module and pinned to a release. The site itself has no stylesheet; [the case study](/work/aitrien-com/) explains why.

## What comes next

Posts about the pieces that made the rebuild work: keeping the stored theme from flashing on load, and retiring design tokens without forcing every site onto a new major version. Both are already filed under [design systems](/tags/design-systems/).
